Add a wine

You can enter a bottle’s details manually, or take or import a photo of its label. Pernolay uses on-device intelligence to extract the wine name, producer, region, and vintage from the photo.

Find peak drinking window

The wine producer is the best source of advice on when to enjoy a wine. If you can’t get that information directly, Pernolay provides prompts you can use in your favorite AI app to help research the wine’s peak drinking window.

Device Support

Pernolay supports all iPhones running iOS 27 or later, including iPhone Duo.

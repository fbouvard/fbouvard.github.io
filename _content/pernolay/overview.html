Less is more

  • No rosé. Pernolay will never acknowledge rosé as a type of wine so you never have to get annoyed by it.
  • No money anxiety. Pernolay helps you share and enjoy wine, not track the monetary value.
  • No nonsense. Pernolay has no social network and doesn't serve ads. Pernolay pays for itself through a subscription model.
  • No account. Pernolay doesn't require any account. Your data is privately stored in your iCloud storage.
  • No third party dependencies. Pernolay has zero dependencies on any code besides Apple libraries or on any third party services.
  • No weird design. Pernolay's design uses native iOS UI components and almost no custom implementations, making it easier to support for years across multiple form factors.

Compounding increases.

Your initial brew time t₀ and first increment Δ set the pace. Each brew time grows by the same percentage, so the extra time added between brews gradually increases.

bn = t₀ (1 + Δ/t₀)n
n starts at 0 for the first brew.

Shift the future, keep the shape.

When you change a brew time, the difference shifts all following brew times by the same amount. The compounding increases stay the same until your next adjustment.

S = Tk − bk
tn = bn + S  ·  n ≥ k
Tk is your chosen time for brew k. S is the offset applied from that brew until the next adjustment; it starts at 0.

Gonglitea is a tea timer for Apple Watch. It does not require an app account and does not collect personal data for advertising, marketing, or analytics.

Data on your device

Gonglitea saves your timer settings, current tea session, and up to ten recently ended sessions from the past week locally on your device so you can continue brewing later. The app shares timer state with its own watch-face complications and Smart Stack widget through local storage.

Timer alerts, Siri, and Shortcuts

Gonglitea schedules a local Apple Watch system alarm to let you know when a steep is finished. No notification permission is required. Sound and haptics follow your Apple Watch settings. If you choose to use Siri or Shortcuts, those features are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple’s privacy settings and policies.

Data sharing

Gonglitea does not send your tea-session data to the developer, operate a server for your sessions, or include advertising or third-party analytics services. It does not implement cloud synchronization.

Stored sessions

Ending a tea session saves it to the recent-session list. The app keeps up to ten recently ended sessions from the past week. Older entries are removed from the recent-session list. This information stays on your device.

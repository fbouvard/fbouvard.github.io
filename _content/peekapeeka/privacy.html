PeekaPeeka is an iOS Safari extension that displays pixel companions. It requires no app account. The developer does not collect personal data through the app, operate an app-data server, or include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Local storage

Your selected companion and verified collection access are stored locally and shared between the app and its Safari extension. Extension storage provides an offline fallback. Artwork is bundled with the app.

Safari permissions

Website access is required to display a companion on a page. The extension does not read or transmit webpage content, browsing history, or website form entries. You can change website access or disable the extension in Safari settings.

Purchases

Apple StoreKit handles collection purchases, transaction verification, and restoration. Apple processes payment information under its own privacy policies. The developer does not receive your payment card details. The app stores verified access locally so purchased companions remain available offline.

Contact

If you contact support, your message and email address are used to respond to your request. For privacy questions, contact hey@squeaky.systems.

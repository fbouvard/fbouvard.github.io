Enable your companion

Open PeekaPeeka and choose a free companion. In Settings, open Apps → Safari → Extensions and enable PeekaPeeka. On older iOS versions, Safari appears directly in Settings. Allow the extension on a website, then reload the page and wait for a visit. Safari internal pages are not supported.

Collections and purchases

Each of the six collections contains 25 designs, including one free companion. Buy a collection in the PeekaPeeka app to permanently unlock its remaining 24 companions. Prices are displayed in your local App Store currency. Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account to recover access. There is no subscription.
